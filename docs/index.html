PVA

Photovoltaic Art

A solar panel is retired after twenty or twenty-five years.

It is not dead.

PVA finds it new work, as art.

Retired, not dead

A retired panel still turns sunlight into electricity, a little less than it did, every day the sun shines. Thousands are taken down each year from roofs and fields. Many are stacked, shredded or shipped away, long before the light stops reaching them.

Recycling recovers their glass and metal. PVA asks something else of them first: to stay whole, in public, and keep doing the one thing they were made for, catching the sun, for a purpose that has nothing to do with the grid.

A panel on a wall can tell the time with its shadow. It can measure its own light and heat. It can wake at dawn, rest at dusk, and make something from the day in between. Its age is part of the work, not a defect to hide.

Principles
  1. 1The panel is the work.It stays in view, as itself. Whatever is added serves what the panel does with the sun, never hides it.
  2. 2End of life is not the end.A panel is chosen because it has been retired.
  3. 3The panel powers only its own work.It is never connected to the grid. A battery may store its own sun, never the grid's.
  4. 4The sun sets the rules.Works keep solar time. They wake with the light and rest when their sun runs out.
  5. 5Offline by nature.No internet, no account, no tracking. A work may count visitors, but never records who they are.
  6. 6Honest measure.A panel reports only what it can know: light, heat, time.
  7. 7Public.In streets and on walls, open to anyone. The first layer of every work needs nothing but eyes.
  8. 8Slow.Tended on foot, changed at walking pace, cared for over years.
  9. 9An edition of nine.Every public installation is nine panels: never more, never less. Like a print run, the number is fixed: in any town, a PVA work is the same size of work.

Make your own

Every PVA work is published so it can be made again somewhere else. Take the designs, place nine panels in your own town, and run them your own way.

Like the sun that powers them, the works are made to be enjoyed by everyone.
